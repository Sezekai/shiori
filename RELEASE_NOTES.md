# Shiori 1.0.0 — the PC app

The first complete release of the Windows app.

- **Three sections:** Manga, Comics and Art Books, each with its own folders, library, history, stats, tags and save folder. Your files are only read; Shiori never moves, renames or edits them.
- **Library:** cover grid (Comfortable, Compact, Cover only, Staggered) and a sortable list. Manga chapters group into series. Comics can be browsed by folder in your reading order, or by series. A reading progress line sits on every cover.
- **Titles from file names:** artist, circle, magazine, event, language and gallery numbers are read from names like `(C90) [Circle (Artist)] Title (Parody) [English]`. Titles Shiori isn't sure about are listed under *Check titles*, and any title can be edited by hand.
- **Filters and sorting:** read, in progress, favourite, rated, series, rating range, page and size ranges, times read, read recently, tags and artists, each with its opposite. Save any combination as a preset. Random title button.
- **Ratings, favourites, tags:** half-star ratings, favourites, tags with colours and drag-to-reorder (also the titles inside a tag), artist view.
- **Reader:** left to right, right to left, top to bottom (page by page or continuous strip); fit screen, width, height or original; one page, two pages or automatic; cover offset; split wide pages; crop borders; page number; page strip; zoom.
- **Saving pages:** original file, JPG (with quality setting) or PNG, including two pages combined into one image. The same page is never saved twice, even after you move the saved images.
- **History and stats:** every reading session (start, end, time, pages), every completion and read count, plus totals, monthly charts, a reading calendar, streaks and top artists.
- **Next file:** finishing a file shows a short end screen, then moves on to the next chapter or the next title in the list. Already-read chapters can be skipped.
- **YACReader import:** brings in read marks, the page you were on, ratings, favourites, and labels and reading lists (as tags).
- The usual app-suite basics: Appearance screen with theme files, keyboard shortcuts popup (K), log, Storage settings with sizes and Move…, Reset one part at a time, crash log and safe mode.
